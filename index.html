Good to Know

Your questions, answered.

How does booking work?

Add your preferred package to your bag and prepare an email request. We'll confirm availability and payment arrangements before any booking is final.

Is delivery included?

Standard Niagara service-area delivery and professional styling are included in package prices. Additional travel charges may apply outside the area.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily, provided we have safe access to the agreed installation area.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We may reschedule an installation when weather makes it unsafe. We'll coordinate a suitable alternative date.
