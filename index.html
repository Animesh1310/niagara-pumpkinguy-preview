Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, thoughtfully answered.

Can I pay online?

Online checkout is unavailable. Submit your order request and we will contact you to confirm details and arrange payment.

Is delivery included?

Local delivery and professional styling are included for eligible packages within our standard Niagara service area. Any extra charges will be confirmed before payment.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily, provided we have safe and unobstructed access to the agreed installation area.

What if weather affects installation?

We will contact you to arrange a suitable alternative date if weather makes installation unsafe.
